Kintia – Multi‑Cultural Calendar System

Kintia is a powerful cultural calendar app that unifies Gregorian, Lunar, Zodiac, and traditional calendar systems into one clean, elegant interface. Designed for accuracy, clarity, and speed, Kintia helps you explore dates across cultures with seamless navigation and precise astronomical alignment.

Current Features

  • 📅Accurate Lunar Calendar – Includes solar terms and leap months.
  • 🌏Multi‑Calendar Switching – View Gregorian, Lunar, Zodiac, and cultural calendars instantly.
  • 🕒Timezone‑Correct – All calculations use your local timezone.
  • 🔄Cross‑Year Navigation – Smooth transitions across lunar year boundaries.
  • 🎨Clean UI – Modern, distraction‑free interface optimised for iOS.
  • 🔒Privacy‑Focused – All processing happens locally on your device.
  • 🚫No Ads – Pure calendar experience.

Designed for Clarity

Kintia is built for precision and ease of use. Whether you're exploring lunar dates, checking solar terms, or comparing cultural calendars, the interface stays clean and intuitive. Every detail is crafted to help you understand dates across cultures quickly and confidently.

Privacy & Trust

Kintia collects no personal data. All calendar calculations, preferences, and cultural views are processed entirely on your device and never transmitted. For full details, see the Kintia Privacy Policy.

Why Choose Kintia?

  • Accurate lunar and cultural calendar calculations.
  • Clear, modern interface designed for everyday use.
  • Offline, private, and built for long‑term reliability.

Availability

Kintia is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
